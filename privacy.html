Tree Identifier Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Tree Identifier analyzes photographs only when you request a scan. Up to three selected images are resized and re-encoded on your device, then sent over HTTPS through Cloudflare to OpenAI. We send your locale region, month and a broad hemisphere hint to help identification; we do not request GPS location.

Usage, purchases and storage

A random app account token is kept in iCloud Keychain when available. Our server stores this token, verified Apple transaction identifiers and purchase metadata, usage counters, and credit balances to provide allowances, prevent duplicate credits, and restore access across your devices. Payment card details stay with Apple. Generated text results may be retained against a request identifier to recover an interrupted request without repeating the AI call. Collection and History photos stay in the app's local storage.

Ordinary scan photographs are processed transiently by our Worker and are not saved in our server database. Operational logs contain model, token totals, estimated cost, duration and result/error categories, without photographs, purchase receipts, account tokens or other direct personal identifiers. Network addresses may be processed for infrastructure security and abuse protection.

Optional corrections

You may submit an anonymous species correction. Sharing its photograph is a separate, optional choice that is off by default. Only a photograph you explicitly choose to share is retained with that correction for reviewing and improving identification. Keep the feedback reference if you wish to contact support about a submitted photo. Corrections are reviewed before use as evaluation labels.

Providers

Cloudflare provides infrastructure and OpenAI performs the requested automated analysis. We disable provider response storage. OpenAI API data is not used for model training by default; its standard abuse-monitoring retention can include inputs and outputs for up to 30 days, subject to legal and safety exceptions. See OpenAI data controls.

Retention and choices

Usage, purchase and credit records are retained as needed to honor non-expiring purchased credits, manage allowances, and prevent abuse. Optional shared photos are retained for identification improvement; contact support with the feedback reference to request removal. Deleting local Collection and History does not reset server allowances or erase purchase balances. You can manage Apple subscriptions and remove local records in Settings.

Tracking and contact

We do not sell personal information or track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app is not directed to children under 13. Use support for privacy or deletion requests.
