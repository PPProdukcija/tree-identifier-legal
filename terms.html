What the app does

Tree Identifier uses automated analysis to suggest the likely species of a tree from a photograph of its bark, leaf, needles or overall form, together with related information. Results can be incomplete, uncertain or wrong. The app is not a certification, not a botanical authority, and not a substitute for a qualified arborist or botanist.

Safety. Never eat, brew, apply or otherwise use any part of a plant on the basis of an identification from this app. The app makes no claim about edibility, medicinal properties, toxicity or safety of any species. Misidentification of plants can be dangerous or fatal. When it matters, ask a qualified expert.

Accuracy and confidence

Every result is presented with a confidence level. A high confidence is a strong suggestion, not a guarantee. Where two species are plausible, the app shows both. Do not rely on a result as the sole basis for a felling, planting, purchase, insurance, legal or professional decision.
